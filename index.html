Hamster Hunter Discord Bot Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-10-30

This policy explains what data the Hamster Hunter Discord bot (the "Bot", application ID 1502549392492855397) collects about members of the Discord servers it runs in, why, and what you can ask us to do with it.
The Bot is run by Hamuno ("we", "us"), the developer of the game Hamster Hunter. Contact: hamunoobusiness@gmail.com.

This policy covers only the Discord bot. The Hamster Hunter game has its own privacy policy: https://hamunoo.github.io/hamster-hunter-privacy/

1. What we collect

  • Discord profile basics: your Discord user ID, username, display name and avatar, and when your Discord account was created.
  • Server membership: when you join or leave the server, and the roles the Bot gives you (such as rank roles).
  • Message activity: for each message you send in the server, its ID, the channel, you as the author, and the time. We do not store what your messages say.
  • Images and media you post: images, GIFs and media links you post in the server are checked for harmful content (see section 2). We keep a copy of posted images for 14 days and an image fingerprint (a short code that identifies an image without storing it) for images moderators have banned.
  • Moderation records: when the Bot or a moderator takes action (for example deleting a post, a timeout, a kick or a ban), we record the action, the reason, and the post that was removed, so moderators can review it and spot repeat offenders.
  • Leveling and shop: your XP, level, rank, time spent in voice channels, reactions given and received, in-game currency (patties) and items bought in the server shop.
  • Things you send the Bot directly: suggestions and bug reports you submit with /suggest or /bug (these are posted publicly in the server's forum), reports you submit with /report (sent privately to moderators), questions you ask with /ask, and direct messages you send to the Bot (moderators can read and reply to these).

Before 30 October 2026 the Bot also stored the text of messages sent in the server. We no longer collect message text. The older data is kept in encrypted storage, is available only to server moderators, and will be deleted on request (section 6).

2. Image moderation and Anthropic

To catch pornography and scam images, images posted by members who aren't staff are made smaller and sent to Anthropic's Claude API, which tells us whether the image is safe. Images it flags are deleted. Anthropic processes these images only to give us that answer; we do not use them to train AI models.

We also send Anthropic's Claude API activity statistics (usernames and message counts, never message text) to write moderator summaries and to answer /ask questions about server activity.

3. Why we use it

  • To protect the server from raids, spam, scams and harmful images.
  • To run rank roles, leveling, the server shop and welcome messages.
  • To collect suggestions and bug reports for the Hamster Hunter game.
  • To let moderators review reports and moderation history.

We do not sell your data and we do not use it for advertising. We do not share it with third parties except Anthropic as described in section 2 and the service provider that hosts our server.

4. Legal basis (EU/UK members)

Where GDPR or UK GDPR applies, we rely on our legitimate interest in keeping our community server safe and running its features. You can object at any time (section 6).

5. How long we keep it

  • Copies of posted images: 14 days, then deleted.
  • Image fingerprints for banned images: until a moderator removes the ban.
  • Message activity, moderation records, leveling and shop data, reports and DMs to the Bot: for as long as they are needed to run and moderate the server, or until you ask us to delete them.

6. Your choices and rights

You can ask us to show you the data we hold about you, correct it, or delete it. Email hamunoobusiness@gmail.com with your Discord username and user ID. We will answer within 30 days. Some moderation records (for example of a ban) may be kept as long as needed to keep the server safe. If you are in the EU or UK you also have the right to complain to your local data protection authority.

If you leave the server the Bot stops collecting new data about you.

7. Children

Discord requires users to be at least 13 (or older where local law says so). The Bot is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their data. If you think a child has given us data, contact us and we will delete it.

8. Security

The Bot's data is stored encrypted at rest on a server we control, and is sent over encrypted connections. Only our moderators can see it, through a password-protected dashboard. No system is perfectly secure, but we limit access to the people who need it.

9. Changes

If we change this policy, we will update the date above and, for big changes, announce it in the Discord server.
